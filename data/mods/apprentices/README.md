# Apprentices

Colonists learn trades as **apprentices** before becoming experts. Apprentices are formed in the New World only (not recruited in Europe).

Convert progression (field experts and guild apprentices) belongs in **Conversos**, gated by Bartolomé de las Casas.

## Progression

| Path | Steps |
|------|--------|
| European trades | Free colonist → Apprentice → Expert/Master |
| Livestock, Cacao, Vanilla, Tasajo | Same two steps, appended by those mods |

Scout, pioneer, veteran soldier and missionary teaching stay direct (no apprentice step).

## Bonuses

- Apprentice farmer: **+1** grain
- Other apprentices: **+2** (additive) to their goods

## School timing

| Step | Easy trades | Planters / guild | Preacher / statesman |
|------|-------------|------------------|----------------------|
| Colonist → apprentice | 2 turns | 3 | 4 |
| Apprentice → expert | **3** turns | **4** | **5** |
| Total | 5 | 7 | 9 |

(Experience upgrades are random; apprentice→expert is a bit slower than colonist→apprentice.)

## Graphics

Each European apprentice has its own portrait (plus `.size2` / `.size4`
variants). Enable **HD Graphics** last so the painted versions load.

## Compatibility

- Load **Apprentices before Tasajo, Livestock, Cacao, Vanilla and Conversos**.
  This mod replaces the colonist experience and education tables. Those mods
  then append their own paths with `preserve="true"` (dairy, wool, cheese,
  cacao, chocolate, vanilla, hunting, salt and salting, plus convert paths).
  If Apprentices loads later, it wipes them.
- Disable **expandExperience** while using this mod (indoor experience is already covered here).
