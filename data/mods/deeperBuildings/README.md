# Deeper Buildings

Adds late-game colony building upgrades, following the FreeCol 2 idea
[More colony improvement levels](https://sourceforge.net/p/freecol/discussion/719665/thread/45eb9cdf/)
(Misiulo, 2014):

| Chain | New top level | Requirements | Effect |
|-------|---------------|--------------|--------|
| Carpenter → Lumber mill → **Timber mill** | Factory tier | Adam Smith, pop ≥ 8 | 6 lumber → 9 hammers |
| Town Hall → **City Hall** | Civic upgrade | pop ≥ 8 | Unattended 3 / worked 6 bells |
| Church → Cathedral → **Basilica** | Imperial | pop ≥ 12 | Unattended 4 / worked 9 crosses |
| Stockade → Fort → Fortress → **Citadel** | Imperial | pop ≥ 12 | +250% defence |

## Enable

Preferences → Mods → enable **Deeper Buildings**, then start a new game.

Works well together with **Basic Buildings** (level-0 bare colonies) and
**Libertadores** (extra founding fathers).

## Art

Custom isometric icons for Timber mill / City Hall / Basilica / Citadel
(Timber mill generated; others recolored from GPL FreeCol art). Citadel
settlement tiles wrap each nation's GPL fortress tile in a bastioned star
fort with a moat (small/medium/large, plus `.size9` versions).
